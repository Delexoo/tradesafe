FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. Guests can create and join with a temporary display name. You’ll explicitly accept our policies before creating or joining.
Does TradeSafe hold my money in escrow?
No. Demo trades are simulated. Live trades may use a payment provider authorization hold — not a TradeSafe vault.
What is demo mode?
Demo trades exercise the full flow with simulated holds. They are labeled DEMO and never touch real money.
