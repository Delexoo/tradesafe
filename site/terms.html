Terms of Service

TradeSafe is a browser-based coordination tool for private trades between people who already trust each other. It is not a bank, licensed escrow agent, insurer, marketplace, or guarantor against scams.

Accounts and guests

You may use TradeSafe as a guest with a temporary display name and a secure guest session cookie. Display names are labels only — they are not proof of identity. Administrative or payment-provider authentication may still apply where required for security or compliance.

Trades and invitations

Trade rooms are private and link-based. Invitation tokens can expire or be revoked. Access to private messages and evidence requires authorization as a participant after you accept these platform policies and join.

Payments

Any payment authorization, capture, or refund is performed by a third-party payment provider or simulated in demo mode. TradeSafe does not hold customer funds in a vault and does not claim licensed escrow.

Accepting these terms

Creating or joining a trade requires explicit acceptance of these Terms, the Privacy Policy, and the Transaction and Dispute Policies. That acceptance is separate from agreeing to a specific trade’s commercial terms inside the room.
