Terms of service

Effective 22 October 2026 (bundle 2026-10-22-s).

These terms cover the TradeSafe website and private live trade rooms. TradeSafe is a Delexo product. Creating or joining a trade requires active acceptance of these terms together with the privacy policy, transaction and dispute policy, security and fraud prevention policy, and device recognition notice.

The service

TradeSafe is a browser coordination tool for private trades between people who already trust each other. It is not a bank, licensed escrow agent, insurer, marketplace, or guarantor against scams.

Guests

You may use TradeSafe as a guest with a temporary display name and a secure guest session cookie. Display names are labels only — not proof of identity. Platform policy acceptance is separate from agreeing to a specific trade’s commercial terms inside the room.

Payments

Any payment authorization, capture, or refund is performed by Stripe. TradeSafe does not hold customer funds.

Invitations

Trade rooms are private and link-based. Invitation tokens can expire or be revoked. Private messages and evidence stay restricted to authorized participants.

Changes

Material policy changes require renewed acceptance before create or join. We do not silently treat prior consent as consent to a new category of data collection.

Contact

Questions: use the contact channel published by Delexo for TradeSafe support.

Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026 (draft — legal review required).

This page describes what TradeSafe stores for guest and authenticated use.

What we store

  • Guest session identifiers (hashed tokens) and optional display names
  • Trade details you enter (titles, amounts, terms summaries, chat, milestones)
  • Policy acceptance records (policy version, content hash, timestamp, consent bundle version)
  • Security event logs (hashed network references, session refs, event types) for fraud prevention
  • Optional first-party device recognition tokens when you opt in
  • Invitation token hashes (raw tokens appear only in share links)

Processors and sharing

We do not sell personal or device-identification data. Hosting, database, and payment providers process data as needed to operate the service. We may disclose information when legally required. We do not claim that data is never shared with any third party.

Cookies

A secure HttpOnly guest session cookie identifies your session. An optional HttpOnly device cookie may be set only after separate optional consent.

Trade Confidence

Registered, email-verified participants may see a small Trade Confidence card about the other party in a trade they have joined. It may show whether that party’s email is verified (when they have a registered account), the month and year their account was created, a count of finalized non-demo trades on this platform, or that they are participating as a guest.

Trade Confidence is not identity verification, a trust score, or a guarantee of a safe trade. Guests do not receive this feature. We do not show the other participant’s email address, IP address, device fingerprints, session tokens, or raw security logs. Optional device recognition remains for platform security only and is not shared between traders as a “seen before” badge.

Retention

Security events are typically retained up to 24 months; device tokens up to 12 months; consent and admin audit records up to 36 months, unless a legal hold or investigation requires longer preservation. See the Security policy for details.

Transaction and dispute policy

Effective 22 October 2026 (draft).

These policies describe how TradeSafe coordinates private trades. They do not make TradeSafe an escrow agent or insurer.

Trade-specific terms

Accepting platform policies does not accept commercial trade terms. Each participant must review and accept trade terms separately in the room.

Cancellations and refunds

Outcomes depend on trade status, participant actions, and Stripe’s rules for the card authorization. TradeSafe does not hold the money.

Disputes and reports

Participants may open a dispute or submit a security report from the room. TradeSafe may record evidence and status; it does not guarantee recovery of funds or resolution of scams. Reporters see status updates only — not another party’s IP, device data, or internal risk scores.

Invitations and access

Invitations can expire or be revoked. An invitation link alone does not authorize unrestricted payment release.

Security and fraud prevention policy

Effective 22 October 2026 (draft — legal review required). Bundle 2026-10-22-s.

Purpose

We collect security information to protect buyers and sellers against scams, unauthorized access, invitation abuse, payment integrity issues, and disputes. Collection is purpose-limited and access-controlled.

What is collected (required for create/join)

  • Server-observed client IP, stored as a one-way hash and masked form for ordinary admin views
  • Event timestamps (UTC), trade and participant references, session references
  • Security event types (create, join, failed invites, unauthorized access, reports, admin actions)
  • Coarse user-agent family information when present on the request

IP addresses are not definitive identity. Shared networks, VPNs, and carrier NAT are normal. We do not automatically ban users solely because participants share an IP.

Who can access

Authorized security administrators (profiles with admin privileges) may review security logs and incidents. Access and consequential actions are recorded in an admin audit trail. Ordinary participants cannot read raw security logs.

Risk scoring

Internal risk signals (for example, new device token or repeated invite failures) inform rate limits and review. A risk score is not proof of identity and never alone releases or confiscates money.

Retention

Security events: about 24 months. Resolved incidents and admin audit: about 36 months. Legal holds may pause deletion.

Honesty limits

TradeSafe does not claim absolute anonymity, guaranteed identification, or complete prevention of fraud.

Device recognition and security logging notice

Effective 22 October 2026 (draft — legal review required).

Optional device recognition

Separate from required security logging, you may opt in to a first-party device recognition cookie: a random server-issued token (hashed at rest) plus coarse browser family, OS family, and device category (desktop / tablet / mobile).

This is not invasive fingerprinting (no canvas/audio fingerprinting, font enumeration, or cross-site tracking). A matching device token is a risk signal, not verified identity. Devices can be shared, reset, or spoofed.

If you decline

You can still create and join trades. Essential controls (consent, sessions, authorization, invite rate limits, payment integrity) continue. Some “seen before” device signals will be unavailable.

When collected

Optional signals are collected only after you check the optional consent box. They are not collected covertly in the background.
