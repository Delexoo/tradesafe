Terms of service

Effective 22 October 2026.

These terms cover the TradeSafe website and private live trade rooms. TradeSafe is a Delexo product. By using the site, you agree to these terms, the privacy policy, and the transaction and dispute policies.

The service

TradeSafe is a browser coordination tool for private trades between people who already trust each other. It is not a bank, licensed escrow agent, insurer, marketplace, or guarantor against scams.

Guests

You may use TradeSafe as a guest with a temporary display name and a secure guest session cookie. Display names are labels only — not proof of identity. Creating or joining a trade requires explicit acceptance of these policies. That acceptance is separate from agreeing to a specific trade’s commercial terms inside the room.

Payments

Any payment authorization, capture, or refund is performed by a third-party payment provider or simulated in demo mode. TradeSafe does not hold customer funds in a vault.

Invitations

Trade rooms are private and link-based. Invitation tokens can expire or be revoked. Private messages and evidence stay restricted to authorized participants.

Changes

These terms can be updated by posting a new version on this page. Continued use after the new date means you accept the update when renewed acceptance is required for create or join.

Contact

Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026.

This page says what TradeSafe stores for guest and authenticated use.

What we store

  • Guest session identifiers (hashed tokens) and optional display names
  • Trade details you enter (titles, amounts, terms summaries, chat, milestones)
  • Policy acceptance records (policy version, content hash, timestamp, related trade when applicable)
  • Invitation token hashes (raw tokens appear only in share links)

Cookies

A secure HttpOnly cookie may identify your guest session across refreshes. It is not used as payment authorization.

Payments

Payment credentials are handled by the payment provider when live payments are enabled. Demo mode does not process real money.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data for advertising. We do not claim that TradeSafe is licensed escrow or insurance.

Contact

Privacy questions: Delexo TradeSafe support.

Transaction and dispute policies

Effective 22 October 2026.

These policies describe how TradeSafe coordinates private trades. They do not make TradeSafe an escrow agent or insurer.

Trade-specific terms

Each trade has its own details (item or service, amount, roles, fulfillment, deadlines). Accepting platform policies does not accept those commercial terms. Each participant must review and accept the trade terms separately in the room.

Cancellations and refunds

Outcomes depend on trade status, participant actions, and — when enabled — the payment provider’s rules for authorization holds and captures. Demo trades simulate these states only.

Disputes

Participants may open a dispute from the room. TradeSafe may record evidence and status; it does not guarantee recovery of funds or resolution of scams.

Invitations and access

Invitations can expire or be revoked. Losing a guest session may require a new invitation. An invitation link alone does not authorize unrestricted payment release.
